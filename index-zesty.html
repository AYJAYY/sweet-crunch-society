Zesty Garlic Dill Refrigerator Pickles

Refrigerator Recipe & Customization Guide
  • StyleRefrigerator (No Canning)
  • Prep Time20 mins + 2 hr soak
  • ProfileSavory & Garlicky
  • CucumberKirby (Pickling)
  • Yield2 Pint Jars
  • Peak FlavorDays 5–12

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs Kirby cucumbers
  • 2 tbsp Kosher salt (for soak)
  • 2 cups Ice cubes (for soak)
  • 1 1/2 cups White vinegar (5%)
  • 1 cup Water
  • 1 tbsp Kosher salt (for brine)
  • 1 tsp White sugar
  • 1 tsp Black peppercorns
  • 1/2 tsp Yellow mustard seed
  • 1/4 tsp Coriander seed
  • 6 cloves Garlic, smashed
  • 2 whole Fresh bay leaves
  • 6 sprigs Fresh dill
  • 1/4 tsp Calcium chloride

Preparation Steps

  1. Moisture DrawTrim 1/16" off each blossom end, then quarter 1 1/2 lbs of cucumbers lengthwise into spears (cut to sit ~1/2" below the jar rim) and toss in a colander with 2 tbsp kosher salt, top with 2 cups ice, and drain 2 hours in the fridge over a bowl. Rinse briefly & pat thoroughly dry.
  2. Craft Garlic BrineCombine 1 1/2 cups white vinegar, 1 cup water, 1 tbsp kosher salt, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp peppercorns, 1/2 tsp mustard seed, and 1/4 tsp coriander seed; simmer until salt dissolves. Add 6 smashed garlic cloves; simmer 1 more minute. Cover and cool to room temp before using.
  3. Pack JarsPlace 1 bay leaf, 3 garlic cloves (fished out of the cooled brine), 3 dill sprigs, and 1/8 tsp calcium chloride in each jar. Stand raw spears upright and wedge them in tight, tucking garlic and dill between.
  4. Submerge & ChillPour cooled brine over veggies until submerged. Seal tight and place directly in the fridge.
  5. Aging & StorageWait at least 72 hours before eating; spears are thicker than rounds, so the brine needs longer to penetrate. Flavor peaks days 5–12; keeps up to 3–4 weeks.

Customizations & Recipe Alterations

Garlic Intensity

  • Extra Garlicky: Double the cloves to 6 per jar (12 total; simmer all 12 in the brine) for a bold, assertive bite.
  • Roasted Garlic: Roast cloves before adding for a mellow, caramelized sweetness.

Herb & Spice Variations

  • Seed-Forward Dill: Skip the fresh sprigs and use 1 tsp dill seed per jar for deeper, earthier flavor.
  • Peppercorn Blend: Use a mix of black, white, and pink peppercorns for a more complex bite.

Texture & Cut Options

  • Spears: The default cut. Quartered lengthwise for classic deli-style garlic spears.
  • Coins: Slice 3/8" thick rounds for snacking and charcuterie boards; cure 48–72 hours, peak days 3–10.
  • Chunky Halves: Halve lengthwise instead of quartering for extra crunch and chew; allow 4+ days to cure.

Process & Storage Notes

  • Raw Packing: Room-temp brine over raw veggies preserves maximum crunch — salt/ice soak must come first. Skipping it causes shriveling.
  • Drying Secret: Thorough drying post-soak prevents brine dilution.
  • Storage Life: Best consumed within 3–4 weeks in cold storage.
