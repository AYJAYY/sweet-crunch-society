Golden Bread & Butter Pickles

Refrigerator Recipe & Customization Guide
  • StyleRefrigerator (No Canning)
  • Prep Time20 mins + 2 hr soak
  • ProfileSweet & Tangy
  • CucumberKirby (Pickling)
  • Yield2 Pint Jars
  • Peak FlavorDays 5–14

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs Kirby cucumbers
  • 2 tbsp Kosher salt (for soak)
  • 2 cups Ice cubes (for soak)
  • 1 1/2 cups White vinegar (5%)
  • 1 1/4 cups White sugar
  • 1/4 cup Light brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp Kosher salt (for brine)
  • 1 tbsp Yellow mustard seed
  • 8–10 Black peppercorns
  • 3/4 tsp Celery seed
  • 1/2 tsp Ground turmeric
  • 1/8 tsp Ground cloves
  • 2 whole Fresh bay leaves
  • 2 whole White pearl onions
  • 1/4 tsp Calcium chloride

Preparation Steps

  1. Moisture DrawTrim 1/16" off each blossom end, then slice 1 1/2 lbs of cucumbers into 3/8" thick rounds and toss in a colander with 2 tbsp kosher salt, top with 2 cups ice, and drain 2 hours in the fridge over a bowl. Rinse briefly & pat thoroughly dry.
  2. Craft Sweet BrineSimmer 1 1/2 cups white vinegar, 1 1/4 cups white sugar, 1/4 cup brown sugar, 1/2 tsp kosher salt, 1 tbsp mustard seed, 8–10 black peppercorns, 3/4 tsp celery seed, 1/2 tsp turmeric, and 1/8 tsp ground cloves 2–3 mins until sugar dissolves. Cover and cool to room temp before using.
  3. Pack JarsPlace 1 bay leaf, 1 pearl onion (peeled & halved), and 1/8 tsp calcium chloride in each jar. Pack raw cucumber slices snugly into clean pint jars.
  4. Submerge & ChillPour cooled brine over veggies until submerged. Seal tight and place directly in the fridge.
  5. Aging & StorageChill at least 24–48 hours before eating. Flavors peak days 5–14; keeps up to 1 month.

Customizations & Recipe Alterations

Corn Syrup Swaps

  • Commercial brine: Use 1 cup white sugar, 1/4 cup brown sugar, and 1/4 cup light corn syrup in place of the standard 1 1/4 cups white sugar + 1/4 cup brown sugar. Total sweetening stays roughly equivalent.
  • Extra sweet brine: Use 1 1/4 cups white sugar, 1/4 cup brown sugar, and 1/4 cup light corn syrup. This adds the corn syrup character on top of the full original sugar amount.

Aromatics & Spice Add-ins

  • Classic Onion: Layer 1/2 cup thinly sliced sweet onion between the slices.
  • Garlic Note: Drop 2–3 smashed raw garlic cloves into each jar.
  • Sweet Heat: Add 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes or jalapeño slices.
  • Warm Spice: Add 1-inch cinnamon stick or pinch of allspice.

Sugar Alternatives & Ratios

Direct Swaps

  • Cane Sugar: 1:1 for white sugar. Rounder, slightly warmer.
  • Demerara: 1:1 for white sugar. Light toffee flavor, slightly richer.
  • Turbinado: 1:1 for white sugar. Slightly deeper, faint molasses flavor.

Flavor Boosters (1/4 cup)

  • Dark Brown Sugar: Swap 1/4 cup light brown sugar for 1/4 cup dark brown sugar. Deeper caramel.
  • Granulated Panela: Swap 1/4 cup light brown sugar for 1/4 cup granulated panela. Earthy, caramel-like.
  • Honey: Swap 1/4 cup white sugar for 3 tbsp honey. Floral, more complex.
  • Lyle's Golden Syrup: Swap 1/4 cup white sugar for 1/4 cup golden syrup. Buttery, toasty.
  • Muscovado Sugar: Swap 1/4 cup light brown sugar for 1/4 cup muscovado sugar. Stickier, bolder molasses.
