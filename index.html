Sweet European Bread & Butter Pickles

Refrigerator Recipe & Customization Guide
  • StyleRefrigerator (No Canning)
  • Prep Time20 mins + 2 hr soak
  • ProfileSweet & Tangy
  • CucumberEuropean / English
  • YieldApprox. 2 Pint Jars
  • Peak CrispnessDays 5–14

Ingredients

  • 2 large European cucumbers (1/4" rounds)
  • 2 whole White pearl onions, peeled
  • 2 tbsp Kosher salt (for soak)
  • 1/2 tsp Kosher salt (for brine)
  • 2 cups Ice cubes (for soaking)
  • 1 1/2 cups Distilled white vinegar (5%)
  • 1 1/4 cups Granulated white sugar
  • 1/4 cup Light brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp Yellow mustard seed
  • 3/4 tsp Celery seed
  • 1/2 tsp Ground turmeric
  • 1/8 tsp Ground cloves
  • 2 whole Fresh bay leaves
  • 1/4 tsp Calcium chloride

Preparation Steps

  1. Moisture DrawToss sliced cucumbers in a colander with salt, top with ice, and drain 2 hours in the fridge over a bowl. Rinse briefly & pat thoroughly dry.
  2. Craft Sweet BrineSimmer white vinegar, white & brown sugar, salt, mustard seed, celery seed, turmeric, and cloves 2–3 mins until sugar dissolves. Cool to room temp.
  3. Pack JarsPlace 1 bay leaf, 1 pearl onion, and 1/8 tsp calcium chloride in each jar. Pack raw cucumber slices snugly into clean pint jars.
  4. Submerge & ChillPour cooled brine over veggies until submerged. If slices poke above the brine, top up with a splash of white vinegar (5%). Seal tight and place directly in the fridge.
  5. Aging & StorageChill at least 24–48 hours before eating. Flavors peak days 5–14; keeps up to 1 month.

Why European Cucumbers?

Thin skins & tiny seeds absorb sweet brine fast without peeling. Ice-salting extracts excess water to ensure maximum crispness.

Customizations & Recipe Alterations

Sweetness Ratios & Sugars

  • Extra Sweet: Bump white sugar to 1 1/2 cups (1 3/4 cups total) for a heavy syrup style.
  • Floral Notes: Swap 1/4 cup sugar for raw honey or pure maple syrup.
  • Fruity Tang: Swap 1/4 cup white vinegar for cider vinegar.

Aromatics & Spice Add-ins

  • Warm Spice: Add 1-inch cinnamon stick or pinch of allspice to brine simmer.
  • Garlic Note: Drop 2–3 smashed raw garlic cloves into each jar.
  • Sweet Heat: Add 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes or jalapeño slices.

Texture & Veggie Options

  • Thick-Cut: Slice at 3/8-inch thickness for extra crunch.
  • Veggie Blend: Swap 1 cup cucumber for sliced red bell pepper or zucchini.
  • Tannins: Add a black tea leaf or grape leaf to jars for extra firming (optional with calcium chloride).

Process & Storage Notes

  • Raw Packing: Room-temp brine over raw veggies preserves maximum crunch.
  • Drying Secret: Thorough drying post-soak prevents brine dilution.
  • Storage Life: Best consumed within 1 month in cold storage.
